How a piece is made

Four steps. Nothing hidden.

  1. Tell us the job Size, wood, words, and where it will live.
  2. Approve the proof A drawing of the actual cut. Change it until it is right.
  3. We cut it Laser-cut or engraved in the Minnesota shop, then checked by hand.
  4. It ships Packed for the grain, not for a warehouse shelf.
