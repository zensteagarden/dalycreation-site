The work

Prairie geometry, clean lettering, and commissioned cuts. Grain stays visible. The cut does the decorating.

Panels & screens

Horizontal screens, wall panels, and light-catching fretwork sized to a room, a window, or a gift.

Signs & plates

Commissions

A drawing you already have, a place you want remembered, a piece for a business or a house. We send a proof and a price before we cut.

What we don’t do

No dropship blanks. No catalog of identical stock. If it cannot be proved first, we don’t take it.

How a piece is made

Four steps. Nothing hidden.

  1. Tell us the jobSize, wood, words, and where it will live.
  2. Approve the proofA drawing of the actual cut. Change it until it is right.
  3. We cut itLaser-cut or engraved in the shop, then checked by hand.
  4. It shipsPacked for the grain, not for a warehouse shelf.
